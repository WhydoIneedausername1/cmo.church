# Compliance — Meta / Social Ads (free local, practical)

## TOC

1. Testimonials & social proof
2. Before/after & sensitive categories
3. AI disclosure & labels
4. Advantage+ / template tools
5. Creative technical
6. Checklist

> Not legal advice. Encode DNA legal qualifiers; escalate regulated categories to counsel.

## 1. Testimonials & social proof

- No fabricated reviews, star ratings, or “customers say” with invented quotes
- Real UGC needs permission rights
- Do not present synthetic people as verified buyers (this pack should not generate them)

## 2. Before/after & sensitive categories

- Health/weight/finance/housing/employment: high scrutiny — use DNA qualifiers; avoid misleading implications
- Before/after: consistent lighting/framing; include required disclaimers; no implied guaranteed results

## 3. AI disclosure & labels

- Follow current Meta AI labeling / disclosure requirements when creatives are AI-altered
- Free-local HTML/HyperFrames type overlays alone are not a license to skip disclosure if other AI alteration was used outside this pack
- Prefer staying on free-local path so disclosure scope stays clear

## 4. Advantage+ / template tools

When layout is **intentionally designed** (HTML static-code, custom MG):

- Prefer **Advantage+ Image/Creative Template features OFF** (or equivalent) so Meta does not restyle/re-crop and break brand-exact type
- Still allow Advantage+ *audience/budget* optimization unless brief says otherwise

## 5. Creative technical

- Export exact sizes; avoid upscaling soft PNGs into 9:16
- Keep landing page claim-aligned with ad claim
- Include visible legal when claim requires it (readable, not 6px gray)

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] No fabricated social proof
- [ ] Qualifiers present per DNA
- [ ] No forbidden generative-API produce path
- [ ] Designed layout protected from destructive auto-templates
- [ ] Claim ↔ LP consistency checked
