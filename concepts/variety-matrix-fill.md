# Variety Matrix Fill — 5,000 Pastor Leads offer (2026-09-05)

Demo batch (4 concepts, not a full 5–8 weekly sprint) built from brand's own published
copy + user-supplied hook themes, since no third-party customer-language source
(reviews/Reddit/Skool posts) was available for this pass. See brand-dna.md §6 for
the customer-language caveat.

| Quadrant | Concept | Primary hook theme |
|----------|---------|---------------------|
| Outcome / Proof-heavy | `CostPerLead` | "$3 or less" cost-per-lead stat |
| Pain / Proof-heavy | `MoneyBackGuarantee` | "5,000 leads or get a refund" risk reversal |
| Social-proof / Proof-heavy | `PastorSpecialists` | "We specialize in connecting you with pastors" niche authority + track record |
| Pain / Curiosity | `LeadMagnetFailing` | "Why your lead magnet is quietly burning your ad budget" |

**Selected for `ad-free-video` production this round:** `MoneyBackGuarantee` — it's the
strongest single hook for a cold-traffic 10–15s spot (biggest risk-reversal, matches
the exact page headline "Get 5,000 Pastor Leads in 5 Months or Get a Refund"), and
folds in the cost-per-lead-under-$3 proof point as a supporting beat. The other three
remain as approved statics/backlog concepts.
