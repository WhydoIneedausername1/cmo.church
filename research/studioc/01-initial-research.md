## 3. The ICP is a finite, nameable list, so this is ABM

- **Market size, rough estimate:** Hartford Institute research counts about **1,500–1,850 megachurches (2,000+)**. The "very large" 1,000–1,999 tier roughly matches or exceeds that. **Working estimate: about 3,500–5,000 US Protestant churches at 1,000–7,000 AWA.** Hartford doesn't publish a single count for this band, so firm it up with Outreach 100 / Hartford data and a purchased list.
  - Example check: the SBC alone reported 603 churches at 1,000+ (older data), about 1.3% of its churches.
- **Penetration:** 200+ customers out of about 4,000 is roughly **5%**. There's room to grow, but every account matters, and much of the market already knows StudioC or its competitors.
- **Tailwind:** Hartford's *Megachurch Resurgence* (2026) found that **67% of megachurches are above pre-pandemic attendance** and **84% say they're stronger.** Growth is back, and that's exactly StudioC's "your church is growing, but are your people?" moment.
- **Buying committee (3–5 people per church):** Executive Pastor (economic buyer, StudioC's stated target), Communications/MarCom Director (daily user and champion), Senior Pastor (vision and sign-off), Ops/Finance, and Discipleship/Next Steps pastor.
- **⚠️ Check our own offer:** "5,000 Pastor Leads in 5 Months" is more than the number of churches in the ICP. Reframe it as **"X decision-makers across Y named ICP accounts"** (for example, 4–5 contacts × 1,000 accounts). Otherwise it reads like we don't understand their market.

## 5. Early strategic hypotheses (to pressure-test with Crystal)

### A. Market access & awareness
1. **Partner channel first.** Turn Pushpay and Ministry Brands from listings into lead programs: co-marketing webinars, partner rep enablement, presence at their user events, and co-sell spiffs. Add Subsplash, Planning Center and Rock (Rock's community conference) as integration partners. This is likely the cheapest pipeline available.
2. **Own the XP room.** Target executive-pastor gatherings over big general conferences:
   - **XP Summit 2027:** May 3–5, Colorado (Flatirons). Sponsor and speak.
   - **WinShape Executive Pastor Collective:** aimed at churches with **1,000–3,000 AWA**, an exact ICP match.
   - **Unstuck Group XP cohorts** (Blue has written for Unstuck, so a warm relationship likely exists), XPastor seminars, EFCA XP Gathering, NACBA (church admin/finance).
   - Selective bigger stages: **Exponential** (March 2027, Orlando), **ARC** (late April 2027), Global Leadership Summit (August).
   - Comms-director track: Church Communications community, UMAC 2027, Baptist Communicators (2027, Pittsburgh).
3. **A research flagship.** Publish an annual **"State of Church Engagement in Large Churches"** report, ideally with Hartford/Warren Bird or with Barna (though Barna is Gloo-owned). StudioC's ChurchIQ data across 200+ churches is a unique asset. It earns press, speaking slots and gated leads, and it supports the claim to own the category.
4. **Customer-led XP roundtables.** Regional, invite-only dinners or half-days hosted at customer churches (Highlands, 12Stone, North Coast, Rock City). Peers selling to peers works best in this market.
5. **Consultant and denominational networks.** Unstuck Group, Vanderbloemen, Auxano, Church Fuel, and large-church networks (ARC, Assemblies, SBC large-church, Converge, EFCA). Set up referral and co-delivery arrangements.

### B. Campaigns & messaging
- **Category narrative:** "Engagement is the new scoreboard." Attendance is back, but discipleship isn't automatic. Tie it to the Hartford rebound data.
- **Translate the method into buyer outcomes:** XP outcomes (retention, volunteers, groups, giving health, one scoreboard) and comms director outcomes (time back, personalization without more staff).
- **Proof-led creative:** Lead with marquee logos and quantified case studies. Right now there are no ROI numbers in the text on their site, which is a gap to close in Month 1.
- **Paid:** Use LinkedIn and Meta matched audiences against the named-account list. Run retargeting plus a high-value offer (an engagement benchmark or free "ChurchIQ health check" built from their ChMS data) rather than cold demo asks.
- **Nurture:** Turn StudioBrief and XP Briefing into a real long-cycle nurture for the buying committee, with separate tracks for the XP, comms director and senior pastor.

### C. Sales pipeline
- **Tier the accounts:** Tier 1 (about 300 best-fit accounts: 1:few, personalized outreach, events, roundtables); Tier 2 (about 1,000: programmatic ABM); Tier 3 (the remainder: partner and content).
- **Define stages together:** Engaged account → MQA (multiple contacts active) → Discovery → Workshop/Presentation → Proposal. The Strategy Workshop could double as a paid "foot in the door" offer.
- **Timing:** Church budgets are often set on calendar-year or July fiscal years. Plan pushes around budget season.
- **Instrumentation:** CRM hygiene, account-level intent, and enrichment (Clay, Apollo) so the 2–3 person sales team works the hottest accounts.
